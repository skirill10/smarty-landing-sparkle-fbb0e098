Refund Policy for Smartytel.com

Effective date: 7 August 2026

At Smartytel.com, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We understand that there may be circumstances where you may want to request a refund. This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds will be issued and the process for requesting one. Please read this policy carefully to ensure you understand your rights and obligations.

1.General Conditions for Refunds

Refunds will be considered under the following circumstances:

  • Service Not Delivered: If a service you have purchased is not delivered within the agreed-upon timeframe.
  • Technical Issues: If technical issues on our end prevent you from using the service and we are unable to resolve the issue within a reasonable time.
  • Unauthorized Charges: If there are unauthorized transactions on your account, we will investigate and issue a refund if the claim is valid.
  • Service Cancellation: If you cancel the service within the eligible refund window as specified below.

2.When Refunds Are Not Issued

Refunds will not be issued under the following circumstances:

  • Issues caused by the customer's hardware, software, or internet service provider.
  • Failure to use the service due to customer negligence or misunderstanding of the service features.
  • Dissatisfaction based on subjective preferences (e.g., “I didn't like it”).
  • Refund requests made outside the eligible refund window.

3.Refund Eligibility

The eligibility for refunds depends on the type of service purchased.

  • Subscription services: Refunds are available for subscriptions canceled within 7 days of the initial purchase, provided the service has not been extensively used. Prorated refunds are not available for partially used subscription periods after the initial 7-day window.
  • One-time purchases: Refunds for one-time purchases are available within 7 days of purchase if the service has not been fully delivered or consumed.
  • Promotions and discounts: Services purchased during promotional periods or at a discounted rate are non-refundable unless explicitly stated otherwise in the promotional terms.

4.Refund Process

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact support: Reach out to our customer support team via email at support@smartytel.com or through the support portal on our website.
  • Provide necessary information: Include your order number, the reason for the refund request, and any evidence supporting your claim (e.g., screenshots of errors, payment confirmation).
  • Await review: Our team will review your request and respond within 10 business days.
  • Refund decision: If approved, the refund will be processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

5.Exceptions

  • Force majeure: Refunds will not be issued for interruptions or issues caused by events beyond our control, such as natural disasters, government actions, or other force majeure events.
  • Third-party services: Refunds are not available for services or features provided by third-party vendors integrated with Smartytel.com.

6.Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, you may escalate the issue by contacting our dispute resolution team at disputes@smartytel.com. We are committed to resolving disputes fairly and transparently.

7.Changes to this Policy

Smartytel.com reserves the right to update or modify this refund policy at any time. Any changes will be communicated through our website, and it is the customer's responsibility to review this policy periodically.

If you have any questions about this refund policy, please contact us at support@smartytel.com for clarification.

Thank you for choosing Smartytel.com. We are committed to providing you with reliable and high-quality services.
